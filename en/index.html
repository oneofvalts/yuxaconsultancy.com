About me

I’m Abdullah Uyu. I graduated in mathematics from Galatasaray University in 2025, where teaching is in French, and I’m now a master’s student there. I speak Turkish, French and English.

I’ve been programming since 2015 (Python, C, C++, Scheme). For my graduation project I formalized Desargues’s theorem in Lean. I’ve worked as a mathematician consultant at a cyber-security research company and as a programmer consultant on AI applications at Ziraat Teknoloji, building agentic workflows in n8n.

I’ve typeset dozens of documents in LaTeX. This site and everything I print are set in Go; for mathematics I made Go Math.

Questions

Will you do my homework or exam?

No. I don’t do graded homework, projects or exams; I teach the subject and prepare you with similar problems.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer (IBAN/FAST) before the lesson, or in cash at an in-person lesson. Clinic and workshop seats are confirmed on payment.

Can I cancel?

Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 24 hours before; later cancellations are charged.

Where do lessons happen?

Online (Google Meet), or in person in Beşiktaş, Kadıköy, Üsküdar and Pendik–Tuzla. For in-person lessons I recommend 90-minute blocks.

In which language?

Turkish, French or English. For Galatasaray courses the terms stay in French and the explanations come in the language you prefer.

Pupils under 18?

I agree everything with a parent; in-person lessons happen at home with a parent around, or online.

My personal data?

I use your contact details only to arrange lessons and never share them.
