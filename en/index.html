Questions

Will you do my homework or exam?

No. We don’t do graded homework, projects or exams; we teach the subject and prepare you with similar problems.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer (IBAN/FAST) before the lesson, or in cash at an in-person lesson. Clinic and workshop seats are confirmed on payment.

Can I cancel?

Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 24 hours before; later cancellations are charged.

Where do lessons happen?

Online (Google Meet), or in person in Beşiktaş, Kadıköy, Üsküdar and Pendik–Tuzla. For in-person lessons we recommend 90-minute blocks.

In which language?

Turkish, French or English. For Galatasaray courses the terms stay in French and the explanations come in the language you prefer.

Pupils under 18?

We agree everything with a parent; in-person lessons happen at home with a parent around, or online.

My personal data?

We use your contact details only to work with you and never share them.
