Abdullah Uyu

Mathematics and computing lessons, LaTeX typesetting, automation

Abdullah graduated in mathematics from Galatasaray University in 2025, where teaching is in French, and is now a master’s student there. He speaks Turkish, French and English.

He has been programming since 2015 (Python, C, C++, Scheme); for his graduation project he formalized Desargues’s theorem in Lean. He has worked as a mathematician consultant at a cyber-security research company and as a programmer consultant on AI applications at Ziraat Teknoloji, building agentic workflows in n8n.

He has typeset dozens of documents in LaTeX and made a mathematics typeface, Go Math.
